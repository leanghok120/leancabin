---
title: "Insigficance of life in the grand scheme of the universe"
date: 2026-09-17
---

Isn’t it crazy how insignificant our presence is in the scale of the
cosmos.

It’s crazy to think that at some point in time, for a little moment,
life evolved on a tiny planet.

Our existence in this universe is completely random. We have no reasons
to exist and yet we do.

We are mere specks of dust that will inevitably return back to the
universe.

This doesn’t necessarily mean that our lives are meaningless. In fact, I
believe that this is what makes life so special.
