---
title: "The greatest gift is the gift to have never existed"
date: 2026-09-05
---

I believe that bringing a human into this world is immoral.

My reason for this is that human beings are subjected to inevitable
suffering and pleasure. Between existing in an unpredictable world
filled with pain and having never existed at all, I would rather the
latter. This is because to deny a person of pleasure isn't morally bad
if the person does not feel. While on the other hand, bringing a person
into this world knowing that that person is going to experience at least
some pain is morally bad.
