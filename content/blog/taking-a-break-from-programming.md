---
title: "Taking a break from programming"
date: 2025-03-02
---

Lately, programming feels more like a chore and not a hobby that I would
do for fun in my free time. I can’t even bring myself to open up my
terminal now (alt+enter) and even if I do, I just don’t know what to
work on. I’ve tried to rediscover the joy of programming by trying out
different tech stacks and trying out low level programming but it just
doesn’t feel the same anymore.

I’m sure this is just another case of burn out which is why I decided to
take a small break from programming to mess around with blogging and
badminton (my new found hobby). I hope that maybe after a month or two
when I come back to programming, there would be something new that would
excite me.

For now, I’d just like to take some time off from programming and just
enjoy the wonderful things that are here on Earth.
